## Explanation

Yesterday's explanation ended by naming a debt: 2.021 asserts that objects
form the world's substance and therefore cannot be compound, but it does not
say what breaks if that were false. 2.0211 is where the debt starts getting
paid. "Hätte die Welt keine Substanz, so würde, ob ein Satz Sinn hat, davon
abhängen, ob ein anderer Satz wahr ist." If the world had no substance, then
whether a proposition had sense would depend on whether another proposition
was true.

Two words are doing the load-bearing work here, and they are not
interchangeable: *Sinn* (sense) and *Wahrheit* (truth). A proposition has
sense when it says something — when it describes a possible state of
affairs, a way the world could be. Whether it has sense is settled before
and apart from any question of whether things actually are that way.
"Sense" is about what a sentence would mean if it were true or if it were
false; "truth" is about which of those two it happens to be. A proposition
you have never checked, never verified, can still have sense — that is
exactly what makes checking it a coherent thing to do.

Now suppose there is no substance: no fixed simple objects underneath the
world's complexes, only complexes built from further complexes, all the way
down, with nothing to stop the disassembly. Then a sentence naming or
describing anything could only be pinned to a determinate content by way of
further sentences describing how the relevant complex is, in fact, put
together right now — and those are sentences whose truth you would have to
already know. Sense would stop being available for free, in advance,
independent of the facts, and would instead have to wait on some other
proposition turning out true. That is the exact inversion 2.0211 names:
whether *this* sentence says anything determinate would hang on whether
*that* one does. You could never get sense fixed first and ask about truth
second, because sense would already be asking a truth-question underneath
it. Substance is what keeps those two apart; without it, they collapse into
each other.

2.0211 is one half of the reductio. The other half — what this would do to
the very possibility of picturing the world, true or false — is 2.0212's
job, tomorrow.

## Method

Split 2.0211 from 2.0212 rather than bundling the pair as one day, even
though they share a single antecedent ("if the world had no substance") and
2.0212 continues with "es wäre *dann*" — pointing straight back at that same
hypothesis. The bundling test in ROUTINE.md is whether splitting would make
a day meaningless, not whether two theses are related. 2.0211 clears that
bar on its own: it is a complete, self-standing claim — sense stops being
independent of truth — with its own argument, not a fragment that only
parses once 2.0212 is added. 2.0212 draws a second, further consequence
from the same hypothesis (no picture of the world at all, true or false);
it deserves its own day for the same reason 2.021 got one apart from 2.02.

No interpretive-war note today. 2.021 (day 23) already located the
traditional/resolute divergence at exactly this stretch of argument — is
the regress-to-substance a demonstration the reader is meant to accept, or
a rung performing demonstration — and 2.0211 is the promised continuation
of that same argument, not a new passage where the two readings part ways
differently. Repeating the note here would restate yesterday's, which
METHOD's own rule (silence where the readings don't newly diverge) counts
against, not for.

No Zen note today either, and for a reason worth stating plainly rather
than leaving as an unexplained gap: the regress-of-justification shape here
— can a claim's very intelligibility be made to depend on some other
claim's truth, without vicious circularity — has real cross-tradition
material (Nāgārjuna's regress arguments, Madhyamaka's own hunt for
something that doesn't depend on something else, reach immediately to
mind). But this project used Buddhist-family material twice in the last
four days (day 21, day 23), the second of those explicitly as the scheduled
payment of an old debt rather than a fresh reach. A third reach in four
days, with no dated debt forcing it, is exactly the reflexive-default
pattern the day 10–11 diversity discipline exists to catch. Silence is the
correct call, not a failure to find something.

Day 9's glossary tripwire (simple, substance, unalterable landing on
"object" in quick succession) stays at two of three; 2.0211 predicates
nothing new of *object* — its subject is *Satz* (proposition) — so it does
not move that count. Still held for 2.027.

No app or presentation change. Conditional theses ("if... then...") are new
in form but not in typographic demand — the existing paragraph shape
carries this one without strain, and nothing about a two-part reductio
spread across two days asks anything new of the single-thesis-per-page
layout. No destruction argument owed.
