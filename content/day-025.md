## Explanation

Yesterday's thesis named the first casualty of a world without substance:
sense would stop being fixed in advance and start depending on truth,
collapsing an order that is supposed to run the other way. "Es wäre dann
unmöglich, ein Bild der Welt (wahr oder falsch) zu entwerfen." It would
then be impossible to form a picture of the world (true or false). Same
hypothesis, same "then" — but now the harsher consequence.

Notice what the parenthesis is doing. It does not say a picture would be
unreliable, or hard to verify, or likely to come out false. It says *true
or false* — either one, without preference — would be equally out of
reach. That marks this as a different kind of failure than the sense
worry. A false picture is still a picture: it lays out a determinate way
things could be and simply misses how things are. If the trouble were
only about getting things right, false pictures would survive the
trouble and true ones would be the casualty. Instead both go together,
which means the failure sits underneath the true/false distinction
entirely, at the point where a picture gets drafted (*entwerfen* — to
lay out, to project) in the first place, before anyone asks whether it
matches anything.

The two theses now read as one argument in two blows. 2.0211: without
substance, a sentence's content can't be pinned down except by way of
further sentences whose truth you'd already have to know — sense stops
being available in advance. 2.0212: press that failure back to its root
and it takes picturing itself down with it, not just some pictures. You
cannot lay out a determinate possible arrangement of the world at all if
there is nothing determinate to lay it out *of*. This is also the first
appearance of "Bild" — picture — anywhere in this book. It arrives here
as a casualty in a reductio, not yet as the positive doctrine; what a
picture *is*, and what it takes for one to represent, is 2.1's question,
not today's.

With 2.0212 the reductio that opened at 2.02 closes. Objects must be
simple (2.02) because they form the world's substance (2.021); deny that
substance and sense collapses into truth (2.0211) and picturing
collapses entirely (2.0212). Four days, one argument, now complete.

## Zen

Kant's Transcendental Deduction makes a structurally close move.
Representations that merely arrive, unconnected, are "nothing to me" —
Kant's phrase for representations that have not yet been bound together
under the unity of apperception, the "I think" that he argues must be
able to accompany all of them. Without that unifying act, nothing would
count as a representation of an object at all, and judgment — affirming
or denying, true or false alike — would have no material to work on.
Same shape as 2.0212: strip out the unifying condition and you lose not
just correct judgments but the very possibility of judging, true or
false together.

The disanalogy is where the unity sits. Kant's is transcendental in the
strict sense — a condition the *knowing subject* supplies, the mind's
contribution without which experience could not be synthesized into
objects at all. Wittgenstein's substance is not a subject's achievement;
it is what the *world* would have to already contain, independent of any
mind picturing it, for picturing to find something determinate to latch
onto. Kant asks what thought must bring; 2.0212 asks what the world must
already have. Both run the same reductio — strip the condition and even
a false judgment becomes impossible — pointed in opposite directions.

## Method

The four-day arc closes today, and the honest self-critique is about
that arc's shape rather than about 2.0212 taken alone. 2.02 through
2.0212 have now run as four single-thesis days on what is, structurally,
one argument with four steps. ROUTINE's bundling clause asks whether
splitting would make a day meaningless — it wouldn't have, at any of the
four joints, because each step states a distinct claim with its own
argument (objects are simple; objects are substance; sense would
collapse; picturing would collapse). Four correct calls in a row on the
same question is still worth flagging as a pattern rather than let pass
silently: the test held up every time it was asked, which is some
evidence the test is calibrated right for reductio chains generally, not
just charitable in this one case.

No interpretive-war note, for the third consecutive day. Day 23 located
the traditional/resolute divergence once, at 2.021 itself — is the
regress-to-substance a demonstration the reader is meant to accept, or a
rung performing demonstration on the way to being thrown away. 2.0211
and 2.0212 both continue that same argument rather than opening new
ground, so the divergence already on record covers them without
restating. Worth checking again at 2.022, where the argument's frame
changes (imagined worlds sharing form with the real one) rather than
continuing this reductio.

Day 9's glossary tripwire (simple, substance, unalterable landing on
