## Explanation

"Die Form ist die Möglichkeit der Struktur." The form is the possibility
of the structure.

Day 41 named this exact debt on its way out the door: 2.032 gave
*Struktur* its name — an atomic fact's actual, realized combination —
and left "how the two words relate... is 2.033's sentence, not this
one." Today pays it, and pays it by reaching back further than
yesterday, past the whole 2.03–2.032 run to 2.0141, where *Form* first
entered the book: "the possibility of its occurrence in atomic facts is
the form of the object." That sentence already used the word
*Möglichkeit*, possibility. 2.033 uses it again, the same word, and
simply attaches it to the name 2.032 coined. Form was always a
possibility. Today says explicitly which one: it is the possibility of
Struktur.

Put the two definitions side by side and 2.033 nearly derives itself.
An object's Form (2.0141) is the fixed range of atomic facts it could
occur in. A fact's Struktur (2.032) is the one determinate way its
objects actually hang together, should that fact obtain. 2.033 states
the connective tissue between them: Struktur is not some further fact
standing beside Form, discoverable by separate means. It is what Form
was always possibility *of* — one member, picked out as realized, of
the very range Form already fixed. Neither word changes meaning today.
What changes is that the book now says, in one line, that they were
never two separate topics: a fixed possibility-space, and a point
inside it that happens to be actual.

The definite article is worth a beat, because on its own it can
mislead. "Die Möglichkeit der Struktur" reads, at a glance, as though
Form were the possibility of one particular structure — as if an
object's Form pointed at a single combination in advance. Nothing
earlier licenses that. 2.0141 called Form "all its possibilities of
occurrence" (2.0123's phrase), plural throughout; 2.023 said the same
of a fact's form. What 2.033 actually claims is narrower and more
careful than the surface grammar suggests: Form is the possibility of
there being a Struktur at all — of some determinate combination or
other holding, whichever one a given fact happens to realize — not the
possibility of any one combination named ahead of time. The next
sentence, 2.034, moves the same word up a floor ("the structure of the
fact consists of the structures of the atomic facts"), and needs Form
to keep meaning the general capacity, not a pre-selected combination, or
the climb from atomic fact to fact would have nothing to stand on.

## Zen

Aristotle's potentiality and actuality (*dynamis* and *energeia*,
*Metaphysics* Θ) have already been checked against this book's *Form*
twice, and rejected twice: day 20, against 2.0141's bare definition;
day 33, against 2.025's form/content pairing, on the harder ground of
shared vocabulary (form paired with something namable as content,
matching Aristotle's own form paired with matter). 2.033 is harder
ground still, because it is the first sentence to use the actual word
the comparison trades on — *Möglichkeit*, possibility — to state
exactly the relation potency and act exist to describe: a fixed
capacity, and one determinate thing realized from it. If any sentence
in this run was going to vindicate the comparison, it is this one.

It still doesn't. Aristotelian potency is a capacity *for change* —
*dynamis* is defined by what a thing can become through a process,
*kinesis*, a substrate literally becoming other than it was (*Physics*
III.1: motion is "the actuality of what is potentially, in so far as it
is such"). An acorn's potency to be an oak is spent, over time, in the
oak's coming to be. Wittgenstein's Form has no such history. It is not
a capacity a fact moves through time to fulfil; it is a standing,
timeless range of combinations, all equally available at once, with no
privileged direction of travel and no substrate that changes into
anything. Struktur does not happen *to* Form the way an oak happens to
an acorn — a fact simply has, or fails to have, one of the combinations
Form already made available. Obtaining is not becoming; a few sentences
ahead, 2.06 will put reality itself in exactly those terms, existence
and non-existence, not coming-to-be and ceasing-to-be. The word is
shared, possibility for possibility. What the word names is not:
Aristotle's pair needs change to do its work; Wittgenstein's needs
none.

## Method

Third pass at Aristotelian potency/actuality against this book's
*Form* — day 20 rejected it at 2.0141, day 33 reopened and rejected it
again at 2.025 on harder vocabulary grounds, and 2.033 reopened it a
third time on the hardest ground of the three: the literal word
*Möglichkeit* doing exactly the job *dynamis* is usually glossed as
doing. Checked honestly rather than assuming the streak of two prior
rejections settles a third occasion — the doubt test applied fresh,
same as day 33 applied it to day 20's verdict. The verdict holds a
third time, and for the sharpest reason yet: not a different kind of
form (day 20), not a missing potentiality/actuality structure at all
(day 33), but the *kind* of structure Aristotle's actually needs
(kinesis, a substrate really changing over time) against the kind
Wittgenstein's actually has (a timeless range with one member picked
out as actual, no substrate, no change). Recording the three-pass
history explicitly so a future run does not have to re-derive it if
*Möglichkeit* or *Form* recurs again in a context this close.

Checked the interpretive-war rule and concluded it does not apply
today, on different grounds than the last four days used to decline it.
2.03–2.032 sat on the locus 2.01 opened (day 9): whether "objects hang
together" is a discovery about the world or grammar performed on the
way to 6.54, a streak recorded at four days running through day 41.
2.033 is not on that locus at all — it is a definitional connective
between two already-coined technical terms (*Form*, 2.0141; *Struktur*,
2.032), the same family of move day 20 flagged as eventually meeting
real interpretive stakes only once *logical form* reaches
4.121–4.1212's showing/saying argument. That fight is not here yet;
2.033 is still ground-floor definition. Noting the locus shift
explicitly, rather than silently letting the four-day streak count lapse
or, worse, letting a future run assume it still applies to a thesis it
was never about.

Zen-source diversity checked against the last five days (37: Plato, 38:
Composition as Identity, 39: Great Chain of Being, 40: Zhu Xi, 41:
Saussure): Aristotle last appeared day 33, nine days back, clear of the
window — and reused deliberately as a continuation of an existing
three-part thread rather than a fresh candidate, the same kind of
reuse day 33 itself made of day 20's rejected comparison.

No app or presentation change. Considered a small diagram — a field of
possible points with one marked realized, for Form-as-range versus
Struktur-as-member — and declined: the picture-theory chapter (2.15 on,
flagged day 20 as where *Form* becomes structurally central to what the
site does) is the natural place to introduce diagrammatic apparatus for
this vocabulary, and reaching for one now, on a single connective
sentence, would spend that move early on a thesis whose whole content
is that two words already in the reader's hands say the same thing
together that they said apart. No destruction argument owed.
