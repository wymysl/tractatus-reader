## Explanation

2.02 asserted a property and stopped there: the object is simple, four
words, no argument attached. 2.021 is the argument. "Die Gegenstände
bilden die Substanz der Welt. Darum können sie nicht zusammengesetzt
sein." Objects form the substance of the world. Therefore they cannot be
compound.

"Substance" is the new word doing the work, and it earns a plain gloss
before anything else: not stuff, not matter, but whatever a system of
things rests on without itself resting on anything further — the fixed
point analysis reaches and cannot get under. Wittgenstein's claim is that
objects occupy exactly this position for the world of facts. Everything
else — atomic facts, facts, the whole edifice 1 through 2.014 built — is
configuration: objects arranged one way rather than another. Configuration
needs something configured. If that something were itself already a
configuration, disassembly would carry on one level further down, and one
level further down from there, with no floor anywhere. Substance is the
name for there being a floor. And once objects are that floor, the
"therefore" follows in one step: a compound is precisely something with
further parts inside it, something disassembly hasn't finished with yet —
so whatever plays the substance-role cannot itself be compound, on pain of
not actually being the floor.

Notice what 2.021 does not yet do. It asserts the floor and draws the
immediate consequence for objects; it does not yet say what goes wrong if
there is no floor. That is a separate question — what breaks, for sense
itself, if analysis regresses without end — and the book takes it up
next, at 2.0211 and 2.0212, arguing that without substance whether a
sentence has sense would hang on whether some other sentence happens to
be true, and no picture of the world, true or false, could be drawn at
all. 2.021 states the conclusion of that argument one step ahead of the
argument itself, the same order 2.02 used for simplicity — assert, then
justify.

## Zen

Day 2 opened this project by ranking facts above things — "the world is
the totality of facts, not of things" — and noticed in passing how close
that sounded to dependent origination's refusal to found the world on any
fixed thing. But it declined to call the resonance settled, because it
could already see this sentence coming: "Chapter 2 will in fact posit
simple objects as fixed, unchanging substance underneath all facts — the
direction dependent origination refuses to go. Whether the resonance at
1.1 survives that turn is not decided yet." Twenty-one days and one
verdict later, this is that turn.

It does not survive. Dependent origination (*pratītyasamutpāda*) is not
merely quiet about a substance underneath phenomena; it is the direct
denial that one exists. Every conditioned thing arises in dependence on
conditions, without remainder and without a floor — pull on any thread
and there is another dependency under it, not a fixed object holding
still while relations move around it. 2.021 asserts exactly the opposite
structure by name: objects "form the substance of the world" precisely
*because* they are the one thing analysis reaches that does not itself
depend on further configuration to be what it is. Where dependent
origination finds, on inspection, more dependency all the way down,
Wittgenstein finds a point where the dependency must stop, on pain of
sense itself collapsing — the argument 2.0211–2.0212 will make explicit.
Day 2's carried question closes here, not on a discovered resonance but
on a discovered disagreement, exact enough at the substance-claim itself
that no amount of squinting at 1.1's facts-not-things reopens it.

## Method

The main work today was closing a debt twenty-one days old rather than
finding new material: day 2's Zen note named this exact thesis, by
number, as the place its own deferred verdict would be decided, and today
is that thesis. Verified the deferral was genuine before treating it as
settled — reread day 2's content file rather than trusting the journal's
summary of it — and found the language there commits to nothing about
which way the verdict would go, only that 2.021 was the right place to
ask. The answer this project reached, that the resonance breaks rather
than holds, is the more interesting outcome for a project that has drawn
on Buddhist material seven times in twenty-two prior days: a real
disagreement is worth more than an eighth resonance would have been, and
recording it plainly (rather than softening 2.021's claim to keep the
comparison alive) is the more honest use of the note.

This is the second thesis in three days to use Buddhist-family material,
after day 21's Vasubandhu; day 22 used none. On the diversity discipline
days 10–11 set, that would ordinarily be a reason to look elsewhere. Not
today: this occasion isn't a fresh reach for a comparison, it's the
scheduled payment of a specific, dated debt this project made to itself
before the diversity discipline even existed as a stated rule (day 2 is
seven days earlier than day 9, where "substance" was first flagged as
approaching, and nine earlier than day 10, where the tripwire itself was
set). A debt incurred before a discipline existed is not the discipline's
to police; paying it on schedule is not the same failure mode as
reaching for Buddhist material by default. The next open Buddhist-family
question, if one arises, should be judged against day 21 as the more
recent unforced use, not against today.

Checked day 9's glossary tripwire again: three predicates were flagged
landing on "object" in quick succession — simple (2.02), substance
(2.021, today), unalterable (2.027, still ahead). Two of three have now
landed. The explanation above glosses "substance" in its own paragraph
without leaning on a glossary, but the trigger condition was "all three
landed, prose visibly straining" — still one predicate short. Held open
for 2.027, the same call made on day 21 for the same reason, one step
closer to due.

Also checked METHOD's interpretive-war rule, and unlike 2.02 (day 21,
declined as no new divergence beyond 2.01's statement of it), 2.021 earns
a fresh word: this is the sentence the traditional reading treats as
Wittgenstein's actual argument for realism about simple objects — not
assertion now but demonstration, the regress made explicit — while the
resolute reading treats the same passage as a rung built to look like a
demonstration, sense-making apparatus the reader is meant to see through
and discard by 6.54, not a discovery about the world's furniture. Both
readings agree on what the sentence says; they diverge on whether "die
Gegenstände bilden die Substanz der Welt" is being asserted or performed.
Stated once, briefly, no verdict — consistent with day 9's original
framing and with 2.02's decision not to repeat it where it added nothing;
here it adds something, because this is the argument itself, not another
statement of its conclusion.

No app or presentation change. 2.021 introduces one new technical term
