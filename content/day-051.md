## Explanation

"Das Bild stellt die Sachlage im logischen Raume, das Bestehen und
Nichtbestehen von Sachverhalten vor." Two clauses, but not two claims —
read the second as unpacking the first. The verb is *vorstellen*, "to
present," split by German word order into *stellt ... vor* with the rest
of the sentence sitting inside the gap. What fills that gap is the
picture's whole job description: it presents *die Sachlage im logischen
Raume*, the state of affairs in logical space, and that phrase is then
immediately glossed as *das Bestehen und Nichtbestehen von
Sachverhalten*, the existence and non-existence of atomic facts.

That gloss is not new vocabulary. It is 2.06, word for word: "Das
Bestehen und Nichtbestehen von Sachverhalten ist die Wirklichkeit" — the
existence and non-existence of atomic facts is reality. 2.11 takes that
exact clause and gives it a new subject: a picture presents *this*. Not
facts in some loose sense, but reality, in precisely the sense five
sentences already spent establishing. "Logical space" is not new either
— it is 1.13's, the space in which "the facts... are the world." So
2.11 is built from two parts already on the table, not one fresh claim:
a picture presents reality (2.06), located in logical space (1.13).
Nothing here is a new technical coinage; the sentence's whole force is
in the recombination.

Ogden's English partly hides this. "The picture presents *the facts* in
logical space" — but *Sachlage* is not *Tatsache*. The word for what
obtains, fact, was fixed at 1: "die Welt ist die Gesamtheit der
Tatsachen." *Sachlage*, state of affairs, is a different word, one this
translation has already rendered two different ways — "state of
affairs" at 2.0121 and 2.014, "circumstances" at 2.0122 — and now
renders a third way, "facts," borrowing the very word that already
means something else. A reader working only from the English cannot see
that one German term runs underneath three English ones, or that here
it collides with a term it is not.

What 2.11 buys, then, is scope, not mechanism. It says what a picture
is *of* — reality, at every point where an atomic fact might or might
not obtain, not only the points where one does — without yet saying how
a picture manages to be of anything: what makes marks on a page able to
present a *Sachlage* at all. That question opens immediately after this
sentence. 2.11 is the address on the envelope, not the letter inside
it.

## Method

Checked for a Zen candidate before deciding against one. Nagarjuna's
catuskoti — for any predicate, four positions: is, is not, both,
neither — is a real structural echo of "Bestehen und Nichtbestehen" as
a space of possibilities a proposition ranges over, and the disanalogy
would have been clean too: the catuskoti's fourth position exists
precisely to exceed the true/false frame, where 2.11's logical space is
built to be exhaustively two-valued, no third option. Declined it on
the project's own diversity discipline (established days 10-11,
restated as recently as day 22): the last five days already used
Buddhist material twice, dependent origination at day 47 and
Vasubandhu's *vijnaptimatrata* at day 50, and Nagarjuna specifically
sits closer to day 47's territory (both concern how predication behaves
at its limits) than a two-day gap would forgive even without the double
count. No non-Buddhist candidate presented itself that wasn't weaker —
the yin-yang pairing of being and non-being in the Daodejing turns on
non-being as function (the hub's hole, the bowl's emptiness), a
different sense of "not" than a Sachverhalt simply failing to obtain,
and would not survive being doubted by someone who knows the text.
Silence, not filler. Worth recording for later: catuskoti is unused and
is the strongest candidate on the table, should a thesis arrive without
this week's Buddhist proximity problem.

The interpretive-war rule was not invoked either. Day 50 already named
2.1 as the sharpest traditional/resolute fork so far — the standing of
the whole picture-theory apparatus once the ladder is climbed — and
2.11 does not open a new local disagreement of its own; it is technical
elaboration inside a fork already stated. Invoking the split again here
would be the fourth-in-six-days pattern day 50 itself checked and only
narrowly cleared; today there is no genuine new divergence to report,
so METHOD's own rule (silence where they don't diverge) applies plainly.

No METHOD.md change and no app change. The explanation needed nothing
beyond quoting German alongside English and pointing at word choices
already in the data — exactly the "show, don't say" rule as written,
with no new apparatus. Checked whether the cross-references to 1.13 and
2.06 wanted site support (a link, a footnote marker); they don't — every
prior day has referred to other thesis numbers in plain prose without
hyperlinking them (day 50 to 2.04-2.063 and 4.01, day 48 to 2.061), and
adding linking machinery for two numbers today would be new apparatus
for a pattern the site has always handled as plain text.
