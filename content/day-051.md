## Explanation

"Das Bild stellt die Sachlage im logischen Raume, das Bestehen und
Nichtbestehen von Sachverhalten vor." The picture presents the facts in
logical space, the existence and non-existence of atomic facts.

2.1 said only that picturing happens, and that it is something we do.
2.11 says, for the first time, what a picture presents — and it does so
by reaching back for vocabulary this project has already built rather
than introducing new terms. "Logical space" is 1.13's phrase, unused
since: "the facts in logical space are the world." "The existence and
non-existence of atomic facts" is 2.06's definition of *Wirklichkeit*,
reality, word for word. So 2.11, read against what came before it, says
something quite exact: a picture presents reality, inside the space
where reality's possibilities were already fixed. Nothing in 2.11 is a
new posit; it is the joint where the picturing vocabulary opened at 2.1
locks onto the world-vocabulary chapter 1 and the first half of chapter
2 spent ten sentences building. That is also why 2.11 needs no
Zen note or interpretive-war paragraph of its own today — see Method.

One translation choice is worth flagging rather than passing over. The
German noun doing the presenting is *Sachlage*, not *Sachverhalt* — and
elsewhere in this book Ogden renders *Sachlage* consistently as "state
of affairs": 2.202, two sentences from now, reads "the picture
represents a possible state of affairs in logical space," an almost
verbatim echo of 2.11's own construction. Here, in 2.11 itself, Ogden
instead writes "the facts." The German gives no reason for the switch —
2.202's wording is close enough to 2.11's to make the two translations
look like they should have matched. Ogden's translation is what this
project reads over the "de" text, per METHOD's copyright rule, so the
inconsistency is worth naming rather than silently smoothing over: a
reader comparing 2.11 to 2.202 in English alone would not see that they
share a noun in the original.

What 2.11 buys, precisely, is scope. A picture is not free-floating; it
presents *within* logical space, the fixed field of every combination
objects could possibly enter that 1.13 named and 2.013 through 2.0141
spent constructing. And what it presents, within that field, is not
some further fact about the picture itself but reality exactly as 2.06
defined it — both the positive fact of what holds and the negative fact
of what does not. A picture that showed only what exists, with no
standing to show what fails to, would not yet be picturing reality in
2.06's full two-sided sense; 2.11 insists a picture's reach covers both.
What is still missing — how the picture manages to present anything at
all, what relation its own elements bear to the objects it is a picture
of — is 2.12 through 2.15's work, not today's.

## Method

Declined the interpretive-war paragraph today. Day 50's method note
already placed the traditional/resolute fork "at the door" of the
picture-theory apparatus, on the two readings' disagreement about what
standing the whole apparatus has once the book is finished — not about
what any individual sentence inside it says. 2.11 is squarely inside the
apparatus, fixing what a picture presents rather than opening a new
question about the enterprise; both readings parse it identically, the
same way day 49 found no documented dispute to report at 2.063. Placing
the fork afresh at every rung of 2.11 through 2.225 would misrepresent
METHOD's rule — it asks for genuine divergence, not one flagged occasion
reused as a refrain — so it stays where day 50 put it until a sentence
in this run actually reopens it.

Declined the Zen note for the same kind of reason, checked rather than
assumed. 2.11's two candidate ingredients are logical space (already
carried a Zen note when introduced, day 11) and *Wirklichkeit*'s
existence/non-existence pairing (already carried one at 2.06, day 46);
2.11 restates both without adding a third idea of its own for a note to
be *about*. The nearest live candidate, existence-and-non-existence
sharing one representable field, is the same shape Nāgārjuna's
*catuṣkoṭi* worries at — except that the *Mūlamadhyamakakārikā* is
already this project's most recent Zen source, used four days ago (day
47) for a different doctrine of the same author (*pratītyasamutpāda*,
dependent origination). Day 50's own journal names that kind of reuse as
something to check rather than assume clear; checking it here, a second
Nāgārjuna note inside five days, for a point this derivative of two
already-noted sentences, is exactly the case METHOD's genuineness test
is meant to catch. Silence, not filler.

No METHOD.md change. Today's thesis restates and joins two already-
placed sentences rather than putting new pressure on how this app
explains; the explanation rules, including the translation-inconsistency
flag used before (Ogden's "we make to ourselves" at 2.1, day 50) handled
2.11 as written. No site or build change: 2.11 nests directly under
2.1 with no zero-padding involved, so `site/tree-depth.js`'s parent walk
(added day 50) needs no new case, and `npm test` / `node build.mjs` both
ran clean against the existing code.
