- Explanation reads 2.0121 as the payoff of 2.012's "prejudged"
  possibility, reframed as a claim about *thinkability* rather than only

  naming and setting it aside — day 10 considered *ziran* for 2.012 and
  rejected it as too weak a fit for necessity. Today's candidate,
  Zhuangzi's this/that (*shi*/*bi*) mutual arising from the *Qiwulun*,
  fits 2.0121's actual shape (nothing thinkable apart from the
  possibility of its relation to what it is not) precisely, and the
  disanalogy is exact: Zhuangzi's relativism uses that shape to dissolve
  fixed identity, 2.0121 uses the same shape to complete one, resting on
  2.012's essentialism. Passed the doubt test on the merits, not by
  rotation — no tripwire from day 11 (about reaching for Stoicism again)
  applies, since this is a different tradition doing different work.

  readings beyond what day 9 already stated for this stretch.
