  objects together.
- Zen note uses Composition as Identity (Baxter; David Lewis, *Parts of
  Classes* 1991) — the thesis that a whole just is its parts, not built
  on top of them — as the modern instance of 2.0272's identity claim,
  and locates the same disanalogy that already separated 2.01 from a
  mere heap: Composition as Identity is standardly paired with
  unrestricted, arrangement-indifferent composition (any objects
  whatever fuse into something, disassembled or assembled makes no
  difference to the mereological sum), where 2.0272 requires the
  objects to hang together a particular way — 2.03's "like the members
  of a chain" — for a configuration to obtain at all. First use of
  Composition as Identity in this project; Gestalt psychology's "the
  whole is other than the sum of its parts" was considered and rejected
  for asserting the opposite of what 2.0272 claims.
