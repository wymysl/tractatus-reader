- Explanation reads 2.024 as the definition the 2.021–2.0231 run has
  been assuming without stating: substance is what exists independently
  of what is the case. Centered the reading on "was der Fall ist" being
  thesis 1's own phrase, verbatim — the world is everything that is the
  case; substance is whatever doesn't move when that totality does.
  Connected it back to 2.0211's transcendental argument (what would
  fail without a fixed substance) as the negative shadow of 2.024's
  positive definition, and closed by naming the division of labor the
  whole run performs: chapter 1 is the contingent world, 2.021–2.024 is
  what that contingency has to rest on.
- Zen note uses Spinoza's *Ethics* I, Definition 3 — substance as what
  is in itself and conceived through itself — against 2.024's use of the
  same word. Disanalogy runs on two axes: number (Spinoza's substance is
  necessarily one; 2.024's is irreducibly many objects) and independence
  from what (Spinoza's substance needs nothing else at all, not even for
  being conceived; Wittgenstein's objects are independent only of which
  facts obtain, and remain fully dependent, per 2.0122, on the
  possibility of combining with others for occurring at all). Confirmed
  by grep that Spinoza's day-14 appearance was for *scientia intuitiva*,
  unrelated to substance as a term of art — today is the first use of
  Spinoza's actual definition of substance in this project, and nothing
  in prior days had flagged it forward, so there was no debt to protect
  by waiting.
