## Authored

- Thesis 2.11 (`content/day-051.md`): explanation and method note; no
  Zen note.
- Explanation reads 2.11 as a recombination, not a new claim: the
  sentence's verb, *vorstellen* ("to present"), is split by German word
  order around the whole rest of the sentence, and what fills that gap
  is built entirely from two already-placed parts. "Das Bestehen und
  Nichtbestehen von Sachverhalten" is 2.06's own definition of reality,
  quoted word for word; "im logischen Raume" is 1.13's space, in which
  "the facts... are the world." So 2.11 says: a picture presents
  reality (2.06), located in logical space (1.13) — no fresh technical
  vocabulary, all force in the recombination.
- Flagged an Ogden translation seam as the second half of the
  explanation: *Sachlage* (state of affairs) is not *Tatsache* (fact,
  fixed as a term at proposition 1), but Ogden renders *Sachlage* three
  different ways across the book so far — "state of affairs" (2.0121,
  2.014), "circumstances" (2.0122), and now, at 2.11, "facts" — borrowing
  the very word that already means something else. Noted this is
  invisible to an English-only reader: one German term running under
  three English ones, colliding here with a term it isn't.
- Closed with what the sentence does not yet do — say how a picture
  manages to present anything, only that it does and what it's of —
  reserving mechanism for 2.12 (model) and 2.13 (element-object
  correspondence), following the same device day 50 used for 2.1.
