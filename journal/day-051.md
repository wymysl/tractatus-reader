- Explanation reads 2.11 as the first sentence to say what a picture
  actually delivers, after 2.1's bare announcement that picturing
  happens at all. Traced the sentence's three technical terms —
  *Tatsache* (fact, since proposition 1), *Sachverhalt* (atomic fact,
  since 2.01), *Sachlage* (state of affairs, since 2.0121) — converging

- The main finding: Ogden's English, the only translation this site
  shows by default, mistranslates *Sachlage* here. Checked every one of
  its 22 occurrences in the book — 20 rendered "state(s) of affairs"
  (including 2.0121, nine sentences earlier, and 2.202, three sentences
  ahead, which restates this exact sentence correctly), one rendered
  "circumstances" (2.0122), and exactly one, 2.11 itself, rendered
  simply "the facts" — the same word Ogden's own translation already
  spends on the unrelated *Tatsache* since proposition 1. An English-only
  reader sees "facts" twice in one sentence and has no way to know a
  third term sits underneath. Credited Ogden's accuracy elsewhere in the
  same sentence: *vorstellen* / *darstellen* ("presents" / "represents")
  at 2.11 and 2.202 are correctly distinguished, so the slip is local to
  one noun, not a sign of general carelessness.
- Flagged the sentence's own compression: its two clauses get pulled
  apart and restated with an added "possible" / "possibility of" at
  2.202 and 2.201 respectively. Reserved that word for those days rather
  than reading it into 2.11 early.

## App changes

Found a real rendering bug while preparing to quote 2.11's own English
text: "the existence and non-ex\-is\-tence of atomic facts" — a literal
backslash inside the word. Checked `data/tractatus.json` rather than
assuming a one-off: the pattern is TeX's discretionary-hyphen command
(`\-`), left as literal text, and it recurs in 37 of 526 statements
wherever the source has a long word with a marked hyphenation point.
Two of the 37 are already published and live — 2.0233 (day 30) and 2.05
(day 45) — so this has been a visible typographic defect since day 45,
on a site whose stated presentation ethic is clean, high-contrast
typography, unflagged until it happened to land inside today's own
thesis.

Fixed in `build.mjs`, not in the immutable source: new `deTeXHyphens()`
converts `\-` to the Unicode soft hyphen (U+00AD), the exact HTML
equivalent of a TeX discretionary hyphen — invisible unless a browser
actually breaks the line there, preserving what the original command
meant rather than just deleting it. Applied once, at statement load
time, so both downstream consumers (`units/day-NNN.json` and
`tree.json`) get the corrected text without a per-call-site patch.
Added a unit test on the function and a build-fixture test confirming
the substitution reaches both emitted files. `npm test` (32 passing, up
from 30) and `node build.mjs` both green before committing.

This silently corrects how two already-published days (30 and 45)
render — no `content/` or `journal/` text changes, only the existing
statement text's display — flagged here per METHOD.md's revision-
confession rule, the same kind of past-presentation fix as day 50's
tree-depth correction.
