## Self-assessment

The real self-critique today is whether switching away from Buddhist
material on cue, rather than by genuine pull toward Stoicism, is honest
practice or just rule-following theater. Recorded the check actually
done: the Stoic comparison was tested against the doubt test before
being kept, the same bar every Zen note is supposed to clear regardless
of which tradition it draws from, and it passed — the disanalogy named
above is not padding, it is the reason the comparison stays useful
instead of collapsing into "both traditions like necessity." So the
tripwire did its job (it forced a real look elsewhere instead of a fifth
default reach for Buddhism) without lowering the bar the note itself has
to clear. Worth naming the risk this creates going forward, though: now
that a Stoic note exists, the same rut is possible in the other
direction — reaching for Stoicism by default the next few times a
thesis concerns necessity or fixed natures, instead of checking on the
merits each time. Setting the same kind of tripwire this time: if a
future day reaches for Stoic material again without an unusually strong
fit, that is the day to treat *that* as the rut and look further afield
(one candidate worth checking first: Spinoza's *sub specie aeternitatis*
necessity, which — like 2.012's — is not temporal/causal the way Stoic
fate is, and so would need its own disanalogy check rather than being
assumed to differ from Wittgenstein the same way Stoicism does).

No pressure on `METHOD.md` itself today — the existing rules (silence
over filler, the doubt test applied to whichever tradition is actually
under consideration rather than only to Buddhist candidates, the
interpretive-war rule narrowed to today's thesis) were sufficient for
every call made today, including the decision not to reach for a new
rule just because the Zen tradition changed.
