  flag: 2.03 gave the picture (interlocking, chain-like); 2.031 adds

  translation nuance: Ogden's "are combined" turns something the German
  objects do (*verhalten sich... zueinander*, reflexive — they hold
  themselves toward one another) into something done to them, quietly
  admitting an assembler the German never posits. Flagged, as new

  here to *Bestimmtheit des Sinnes* (determinacy of sense) at 3.23 —
  named as an anticipation only, its content left for that later day.
- Zen note uses Zhu Xi's Neo-Confucian *li yi fen shu* (理一分殊,
  "principle is one, its divisions many") for the shared claim that a
  thing's internal coherence is always one determinate pattern, never a
  vague or interchangeable one. Stated the real disanalogy rather than
  skating past it: Zhu Xi's many li are local faces of a single,
  normatively loaded Supreme Principle (*taiji*), where Wittgenstein's
  many determinate combinations answer to no single unifying principle
  and carry no value — "many moons," not one moon many times reflected.
  First use of Zhu Xi / Neo-Confucian metaphysics in this project.
