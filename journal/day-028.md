- Zen note compares Locke's primary/secondary quality distinction
  (*Essay*, II.viii): a real structural match (a class of qualities,
  colour chief among them, relocated away from the bare object) with a
  load-bearing disanalogy — Locke keeps primary qualities as intrinsic
  and relocates only secondary ones, and relocates them to a
  *perceiver*; 2.0231 grants objects no material property at all and
  relocates them to a *proposition*, with no perceiver required
  anywhere in the picture. Confirmed via grep that Locke and
  primary/secondary qualities had not appeared in this project before
  today.
