- Thesis 2.0231 (`content/day-028.md`): explanation, Zen note, method note.
- Explanation reads 2.0231 as drawing out the cost of 2.023's identity
  (fixed form = the objects): substance *can* only determine form, not
  material properties, and the italicized *kann* marks a limit on what
  substance is capable of fixing at all, not an incidental omission.
  Traced the double *erst* in the second sentence ("first presented by
  the propositions — first formed by the configuration of the objects")
  to show material properties are posterior to combination, not a
  hidden fact about a bare object waiting to be noticed. Flagged that
  *Konfiguration* is the book's own first use of that word, in service
  of a claim it makes explicit for the first time — form belongs to the
  object alone, material properties belong to nothing until objects are
  combined — without importing 2.0232's "colourless" corollary early.
- Zen note compares ontic structural realism (Ladyman & Ross, *Every
  Thing Must Go*, 2007): physical objects exhausted by relational
  structure, no qualitative residue left over once structure is
  described. Disanalogy is load-bearing: OSR's eliminativist wing
  dissolves objects into pure structure with no relata left standing;
  2.0231 empties the object of material content but keeps it standing
  as a substantial individual. Confirmed via grep that neither
  "structural realism" nor "Ladyman" had appeared in this project
  before today.
