# Day 50 — 2026-09-10

## Authored

- Thesis 2.1 (`content/day-050.md`): explanation, Zen note, method note.
- Explanation marks 2.1 as the hinge of the book so far: everything from
  1 through 2.063 described the world on its own, with no mention of a
  thinker or a representation; 2.1 is the first sentence to introduce a
  second thing, us, and something we make (*Bilder*, pictures) of facts.
  Glossed Ogden's deliberately literal "we make to ourselves" against the
  German reflexive *machen uns*, and named two things the sentence does
  not yet claim — that a picture must resemble what it pictures, and what
  pictures are for — reserving both for the sentences (2.11 onward, 4.01)
  that actually make them, per METHOD's one-day-one-thought rule.
- Interpretive-war paragraph: stated the traditional reading (Russell's
  introduction; 2.1 opens the book's real positive semantic theory, the
  picture theory) against the resolute reading (Diamond, Conant; the
  picture-theory apparatus is the most elaborate rung to be climbed and
  then seen as nonsense by 6.54), no verdict given. Judged this the
  sharpest divergence point in the book so far — sharper than 2.01's
  simple objects — since the picture theory is the resolute reading's
  most-cited target, and worth stating precisely at the door of the
  apparatus rather than only once inside it.
- Zen note: Vasubandhu's *Viṃśatikā* doctrine of *vijñaptimātratā*
  ("representation-only") as a structural parallel to 2.1's claim that
  picturing is something we do, to facts, for ourselves. Argued the
  disanalogy is load-bearing: Vasubandhu's doctrine is built to make an
  external world dispensable, once everything a mind has is conceded to
  be its own representation; 2.1 cannot draw that conclusion without
  undoing 2.04-2.063's own establishment of facts and reality as standing
  on their own, independent of any picture-making. Wittgenstein's "of" in
  "pictures of facts" names a relation between two distinct things;
  Vasubandhu's thesis is built to dissolve exactly that distinctness.
  Flagged the reuse explicitly: Vasubandhu appeared once before (day 21),
  for a different, unrelated argument of his — the *paramāṇu* mereological
  regress against partless atoms, twenty-nine days back.

## App changes

Fixed the tree-view depth wrinkle day 9 flagged and set today as its
re-check point, now that 2.1 sits beside its true sibling 2.01 for the
first time. `renderTree()` computed indentation depth by counting digits
after the decimal point — exact almost everywhere, but wrong wherever
Wittgenstein pads a first-level batch with a leading zero (2.01-2.06
vs. 2.1-2.9): the digit count made 2.01 render one step deeper than 2.1,
as its child rather than its sibling.

Surveyed how far the same padding trick recurs before fixing only the
visible case: it repeats at 3.01-3.05, 4.01-4.06, 5.01-5.02, 6.01-6.03
(one whole-chapter batch each, same as chapter 2's), at 3.001,
4.001-4.003, 6.001-6.002 (a second, double-zero layer of the identical
device), and one level down inside the tree at 2.201-2.203 and
3.201-3.203 (a section padding its own children the way a chapter pads
its opening batch). Day 9 rejected a "strip one leading zero" fix because
it mis-nests deeper cases like 2.0201 (which sits under an unlisted
"2.020", not directly under 2.02) and called a correct fix "real new
apparatus, disproportionate" for what was then a single cosmetic indent
on a mostly-dim branch. That judgment was right for day 9; today's fuller
survey shows the wrinkle recurring in five chapters, which changes what
counts as proportionate.

The fix, in new `site/tree-depth.js`: rather than counting digits, walk
a number's decimal part back one digit at a time and stop at the nearest
shorter prefix that actually exists as a proposition in the book; that
proposition is the real parent, at any depth, through any amount of
padding. This is Wittgenstein's own footnote rule — confirmed verbatim
in `data/tractatus.json`'s entry for proposition 1's footnote, quoted
while checking this: "die Sätze *n*.1, *n*.2, *n*.3, etc., sind
Bemerkungen zum Satze No. *n*; die Sätze *n*.*m*1, *n*.*m*2, etc.
Bemerkungen zum Satze No. *n*.*m*; und so weiter" — applied literally
against the propositions that actually exist, rather than assuming every
intermediate decimal position is itself a numbered sentence. No
chapter-specific rule, no hand-built parent table: one general algorithm
resolves every instance, including ones not caught by the survey above,
by construction. Extracted as a pure function (`computeDepths`),
mirroring `site/progress.js`'s existing split between pure logic and DOM
code, and covered directly by seven cases in new
`tests/tree-depth.test.mjs` (chapter-level zero depth; plain digit-count
nesting; the top-level zero-padded batch landing level with its unpadded
counterpart; children nesting correctly under a padded parent; the
missing-intermediate case 2.0201; the double-zero case 3.001/4.001-4.003;
and the one-level-down recurrence at 2.2/2.201-2.203). `npm test` (30
passing, up from 23) and `node build.mjs` both green before committing.

This changes how already-published days render: 2.01 through 2.063
(days 9 through 49, twelve days of published content) all move one
indent step shallower in the tree view, now correctly level with
2.1-2.9 rather than nested a false level under them. No `content/` or
`journal/` file changes — only the tree's own indentation of already-
existing entries — but flagged here per METHOD.md's confession rule
for revising the past, since the visible presentation of twelve prior
days does change today.

## Self-assessment

traditional/resolute split (44, 46, 47, considered-and-declined at 48
and 49) — checked directly whether that clusters into overuse rather
than genuine divergence. Concluded no: 44, 46, and 47 each argued a

last nine days (41-49) by grep: Vasubandhu's specific doctrine here
(*vijñaptimātratā*) is a first use; the philosopher himself repeats from
day 21, argued above as a different work and a different argument, not
hidden.
