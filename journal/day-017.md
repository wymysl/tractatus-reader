  conceivable; a substance with no space to occupy is not). Disanalogy:
  Jain space is an independently existing substance things get placed
  into; Tractarian space, per 2.0124, is nothing over and above what the
  objects themselves already are once given, with no standing of its own
  to fall back on if the objects were subtracted. Also checked and
  explicitly rejected a Buddhist candidate the word "empty" tempted
  (*śūnyatā*) — it denies things a fixed nature, which is the opposite of
  what 2.013's fixed-and-available space claims; noted as a rejected
  false friend rather than silently skipped.

possible glossary widget, once it recurred enough across the book
(2.013, 2.11, 3.4 onward per day 5's own forward citation) to strain

show-don't-say principle applied to the app's own information
architecture, not just to a day's prose. This resolves day 5's tripwire
without building anything new, which is itself the finding: the right
answer to a five-day-old "watching, not acting" note turned out to be
"still not acting," now on record as a checked conclusion rather than a
deferral.
