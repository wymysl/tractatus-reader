worked through, applied to a new axis: whether reaching for a specific
comparative tradition is a genuine, checked call rather than a reflexive
one. Day 15 flagged, without setting a formal tripwire, that two
non-Buddhist Zen notes had run together (Spinoza, Aristotle) — today
made a real check of a non-Western candidate (the *Yijing*'s hexagram
combinatorics) before reaching for Leibniz, rather than skipping the
check because Leibniz was the obviously strong option. The *Yijing*
candidate was a genuine structural echo, set aside honestly because its
disanalogy landed on a numerological counting convention (three lines

underlying picture the way Leibniz's windowless-monads disanalogy does.

three Western days running (Spinoza, Aristotle, Leibniz) — the check was
real this time, but a fourth occasion resolving the same way without an
equally deliberate look elsewhere would be the point to treat the
pattern itself as worth a tripwire, mirroring how day 10 eventually
treated Buddhist-family overuse. This is a soft flag, not a rule change:
