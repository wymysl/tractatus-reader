- Explanation frames 2.034 as paying two debts at once: the near one
  (2.03–2.033's four-day build of *Struktur* at the atomic-fact level,
  now moved up a floor to the fact level) and the far one (day 8's
  fact/atomic-fact distinction — "the fact is the existence of atomic
  facts" — which never said how the two levels' *structures* relate
  until today). Traced the textual echo carefully: thesis 2 used the
  noun *das Bestehen von* ("the obtaining of") for identity work; 2.034
  reuses the same root as the finite verb *besteht aus* ("consists of")
  for composition work — same root, different grammatical job. Argued
  2.034 does not reopen 2.03–2.032's question of what makes objects a
  genuine combination rather than a heap; it takes that answer as given
  and shows structure composes cleanly one level up, with no separate
  glue-structure needed to cross the seam. Closed by explaining why
  2.034 is the right sentence to end this stretch on: 2.04 drops
  *Struktur* for the widest term chapter 2 has, "the world," and 2.034
  is the last stop that shows structure itself scales cleanly before
  that totalizing move.
- Zen note reaches for C. D. Broad's resultant/emergent distinction
  (*The Mind and Its Place in Nature*, 1925), built on Mill's
  homopathic/heteropathic laws (1843): 2.034 asserts, for structure
  specifically, that the fact/atomic-fact case is always the resultant
  kind — the whole's structure is nothing over and above the parts'
  structures, taken together. Disanalogy: Broad's apparatus is built to
  sort *empirical* cases (some resultant, some emergent, decided by
  investigation); 2.034 offers no such sorting, since it holds by what
  "fact" and "atomic fact" mean, with no logical room for a Tractarian
  counter-case the way water was Broad's counter-case to naive
  mechanism. First use of Broad, Mill, and emergentism in this project.
