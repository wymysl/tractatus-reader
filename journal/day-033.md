# Day 33 — 2026-08-21

## Authored

- Thesis 2.025 (`content/day-033.md`): explanation, Zen note, method
  note.
- Explanation reads 2.025 ("it is form and content") against the
  apparent tension with 2.0231 ("substance can only determine a form
  and not any material properties"): resolved by noting *Inhalt*
  (content) is not the word 2.0231 used and does not mean *materielle
  Eigenschaften* (material properties). Grounded "content" in 2.0131's
  own image — a point in logical space as *eine Argumentstelle*, an
  argument-place borrowed from Frege — reading form as the shape of the
  slot and content as the fact that the slot is never, in fact, empty:
  substance is these objects, actually there, not a bare combinatorial
  scaffold. Tied this back to 2.023's identity move (form just is the
  objects, not a separate item related to them) and read 2.025 as
  drawing that same identity out along a second axis: the objects are
  at once what makes the form and what makes the content, two
  descriptions of one substance rather than two ingredients.
- Zen note reopens day 20's rejection of Aristotelian hylomorphism,
  checked fresh rather than assumed to still cover a new sentence, now
  that 2.025 pairs *Form* explicitly with something namable as content —
  the closer vocabulary match day 20 found missing at bare 2.0141. The
  rejection holds on sharper ground: Aristotle's matter and form are two
  really distinct principles (indeterminate potentiality actualized by
  form), while 2.025's form and content are the same fully determinate
  objects described twice, with no potentiality/actuality structure at
  all. Framed as a closer surface match making the real distance more
  visible, not less.
