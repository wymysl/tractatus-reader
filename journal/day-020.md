## Self-assessment

The real methodological point today is about not treating "no Zen note"
as a free pass to skip the check that normally comes with a positive
comparison. Days that *do* find a cross-tradition match (13 through 18)
routinely name and reject a tempting same-tradition or coincidental
candidate before settling on the one they use (day 18's W.E. Johnson,
day 11's tripwire against reflexive Buddhist reaches). A day that finds
*no* match risks skipping that discipline, since there is no winning
candidate to justify in the text. Today did the check anyway —
Aristotelian hylomorphism is the obvious reach given the shared word
*Form*, so it needed a stated reason for rejection (a translation
coincidence, not a doctrinal one) rather than silent omission, which
otherwise reads as not having looked. Worth naming as a general
standard going forward: a Zen-less day owes the same "candidate checked
and rejected" transparency a Zen-bearing day gives its runner-up.

Second, extended the "record a concrete trigger, not a vague watch"
discipline day 19 started for the *Sachlage* concordance question to a
much higher-stakes case. *Form* is not a minor recurring word — at
roughly eighty occurrences and counting, it becomes the single term the
picture theory and the showing/saying doctrine are built on. The
temptation on a day introducing a term of that eventual weight is to
either over-build (some tracing feature, disproportionate to what one
day's thesis calls for) or under-note it (treat it as just another word,
missing how much is riding on it later). Named the actual scale
difference from *Sachlage* explicitly and set a specific future
checkpoint — the picture-theory block — rather than either building
early or filing the observation away without a trigger.
